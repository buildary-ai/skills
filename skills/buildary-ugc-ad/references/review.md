# Review and delivery

## Watch the whole video

In a coding agent you can download the output and look at frames spaced across it (for example every 2
seconds with ffmpeg) and listen to it if you have the tools. In a chat app you may only have the link. Never
claim a check you could not do; list it as unchecked for the owner.

Record each with a timestamp and accepted / rejected / unchecked:

- **Creator:** the same face, hair, wardrobe and body throughout; natural skin.
- **UGC feel:** a believable everyday situation and phone framing; no studio presentation, constant
  grinning, exaggerated shock or announcer voice.
- **Words and voice:** the approved dialogue, language and pronunciation (brand names, prices, units);
  natural pace and complete endings. Kling 4.0 makes its own sound: check that the speech is there.
- **Lip sync and sound:** mouth matches speech; music, if planned, stays behind the voice and ends cleanly.
- **Product:** the right SKU, proportions, materials, logo and label.
- **Handling:** plausible contact and grip, correct hands, a believable camera holder, no duplicated or
  vanishing objects, open/closed states stay consistent.
- **Evidence:** no invented ownership, results or endorsements crept in.
- **Opening and ending:** the product is visible early; the ending is complete, not cut mid-word.
- **Text:** requested captions spelled correctly (Turkish letters too) and readable; no unrequested text.

A technically clean but flat product recital is not a successful UGC ad; say so.

## Fixing a failed video

Name the failed check and the one change you would make (a clearer product reference, a simpler action, a
shorter line). A new attempt is a new paid job: apply the spending rule, and do not exceed retries the owner
approved. Never resend the same prompt unchanged hoping for better luck.

## Changes and continuity

- A regenerated video can change more than the line you edited: creator, camera, product details and sound
  may all shift. Check the whole new output.
- Keep accepted versions; never overwrite what the owner liked.
- For variants, name the one variable each version tests and keep the rest constant in the prompt. Compare the
  outputs for accidental differences. Never claim a variant will convert better.

## Delivery

Give: the link (it expires within an hour; `get_job` gives a fresh one; the file is also in the owner's
library), length and format, the dialogue as it was generated, the credits charged, and the QA notes in two
or three lines. In a coding agent, save downloads in the owner's project under a name you choose
(`ugc-serum-v1.mp4`).
